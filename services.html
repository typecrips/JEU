Brand Launch
End-to-end / from idea to first sale
Launch the brand.
We build the system.

A complete setup for founders who want to build and launch a fashion brand without coordinating every specialist themselves. We connect brand strategy, product development, manufacturing, e-commerce, content and launch into one continuous process.

One package / multiple disciplines
Final result A structured, ready-to-sell brand with products, infrastructure and launch assets in place.
01 / DIRECTION

Brand & Strategy

We define what the brand is, who it is for and how it should operate: positioning, product direction, collection structure, pricing logic, launch priorities and the practical roadmap from concept to market.

02 / PRODUCT

Product Development

We turn the concept into manufacturable products: technical drawings, patterns, materials, trims, custom hardware, treatments, samples, fit corrections and production-ready specifications.

03 / PRODUCTION

Sourcing & Manufacturing

We source fabrics and components, coordinate production, manage small-batch manufacturing, quality control and finishing, and build the production route around the collection and quantities required.

04 / BRAND SYSTEM

Labels, Packaging & Assets

We prepare the physical details that make the product a brand: labels, tags, packaging, custom brand elements, hardware and other customer-facing touchpoints.

05 / COMMERCE

Shopify & E-commerce

We set up the digital storefront and the infrastructure around it: Shopify, domain and email, product structure, payments, shipping logic, fulfillment and the systems required to start taking orders.

06 / CONTENT

Photography & Launch Content

We prepare the visual material needed to sell the collection: product photography, detail imagery and other launch assets for e-commerce, social media and brand communication.

07 / OPERATIONS

Business & Legal Setup

We coordinate the essential business infrastructure with the relevant specialists: business registration, accounting and tax setup, business documents, legal policies, banking and payment systems.

08 / LAUNCH

Go Live

We bring everything together for launch: final product setup, store checks, shipping and fulfillment structure, collection presentation and the practical preparation needed to open the brand for sales.
