About / JEU Manufacture

We build products, not just garments.

JEU Manufacture is a production partner for independent labels, designers and growing brands. We work across product development and manufacturing, helping turn an idea, reference or existing design into a finished physical product.

We can join a project at different stages. Some clients come with a finished technical drawing or collection, while others start with a single idea and need help developing it into something manufacturable.

Production is based in Ukraine. We work remotely with clients internationally and coordinate development, manufacturing, finishing, fulfillment and delivery around the needs of each project.

Who we work with

Independent labelsEstablished or emerging fashion brands developing their own products.
Small & growing brandsTeams building collections and increasing their production step by step.
DesignersDesigners developing individual garments, accessories or complete collections.
Creative projectsCollaborations, limited projects and products that require a physical production partner.
New fashion businessesFounders starting with a concept and building their first products and production system.

We work with people at different stages of building a product or brand. The scope can be as focused as developing one garment or as broad as supporting a collection from its first idea through production and launch.

What we do

We develop and produce garments and physical product details across the full production process. This includes technical drawings, pattern making, sample development, fabric and material sourcing, printing and embroidery, washing, distressing and waxing, custom metal hardware and engraving, brand elements, product photography, fulfillment and shipping.

For brands that need broader support, we can also assist with brand setup, collection launch preparation, e-commerce, content and sales operations.
